Transcript

CycleBalance is a private iPhone app for PCOS and irregular cycles. Track period starts, skipped periods, symptoms, meals, glucose, supplements, ovulation clues, and visible changes over time. Use Apple Health as an optional bridge for weight, sleep, activity, resting heart rate, and glucose context. Build doctor-ready exports while keeping health records on device. CycleBalance is not a medical device; it helps you notice patterns and prepare better conversations with your care team.

What it shows

CycleBalance is for personal tracking and appointment preparation, not diagnosis or treatment decisions.

  • Irregular-cycle tracking without 28-day assumptions
  • Meal, glucose, supplement, symptom, and photo-journal context
  • On-device records with optional Apple Health contribution summaries
