Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

This policy covers the currently available app and the upcoming 1.1.0 update. Changes identified as applying to 1.1.0 take effect when you install that version. Earlier versions may offer different optional features, described below.

The short version

  • Your health logs are stored on your iPhone and are never sent to us. We cannot see them.
  • There is no account and no advertising. We do not sell personal data or share it for advertising.
  • Apple Health access is optional and read-only.
  • Purchases are processed by Apple. RevenueCat checks your subscription using a random ID created by the App and your device's vendor identifier, never your name or email.
  • In 1.1.0, no custom app-usage events are sent.
  • New barcode lookups are paused in 1.1.0. Manual meal entry and previously saved food information remain available.

1. Who we are

CycleBalance is operated by Huggler Holdings LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy covers the CycleBalance iPhone app (the "App") and the website cyclebalance.app (the "Website"). Contact: contact@cyclebalance.app.

2. Data that stays on your iPhone

Everything you log in the App is stored locally on your device, including cycle dates, check-ins, symptoms, mood and energy, ovulation tests and temperatures, pregnancy or postpartum details, medications and supplements, meals and glucose readings, notes, and photos in your private photo journal. This data is protected by iOS Data Protection and app sandboxing. It is not sent to us or to any server we operate, and we have no way to access it.

Reminders (a gentle daily check-in, a period heads-up and supplement reminders) are scheduled on your device.

If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or a computer, Apple's backup may include App data under your own Apple settings. We cannot access those backups.

3. Apple Health

With your permission, the App reads selected categories from Apple Health (for example menstrual and ovulation data, temperature, sleep, activity, and blood glucose) to build your cycle picture. Access is read-only: the App never writes to Apple Health. Health data is used only on your device and is never used for advertising. You can change access at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

4. Purchases (Apple and RevenueCat)

Subscriptions are processed by Apple. We never receive your payment card details. To check which features you have unlocked and to process purchases and restores, the App uses RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives a random app user ID created by the App (not your name or email); your device's vendor identifier (an ID that iOS assigns to our apps on your device, which is not the advertising identifier); your purchase and receipt information; and basic device and app information (such as device model, iOS version, app version, language and App Store country). Connections expose your IP address to RevenueCat. Its customer documentation describes deriving and retaining a country from that address, separately from your App Store country. RevenueCat also retains first and last connection times and the billing and technical information described above, linked to its customer ID. We do not receive your precise location or use GPS. RevenueCat describes discarding the raw IP after country derivation; this does not specify retention for every technical or security log. We use it only to provide and manage subscriptions (including RevenueCat's reports on how many people subscribe, renew or cancel), never for advertising or to track you across other companies' apps and websites. RevenueCat does not receive your health data. See RevenueCat's privacy policy at revenuecat.com/privacy-policy.

RevenueCat’s data processing agreement.

5. Food product information

In version 1.1.0, new in-app barcode lookups are paused. You can enter meals manually and keep using previously saved product information, with its source and licence credits. Earlier versions may still send a barcode and technical request information, including an IP address, to Open Food Facts when you choose a lookup. Your health records and meal diary are not included in that request. If you open a product-source link, your browser visits Open Food Facts and the product code is included in the URL. See the Open Food Facts privacy policy for its processing practices.

6. App events and subscription service activity

In 1.1.0, no custom app-usage events are sent. CycleBalance has no app-usage analytics SDK and sends no custom usage events. RevenueCat records the subscription and connection activity described above. Your health logs are not used for these reports.

If you have chosen in your iPhone settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us crash reports and aggregated statistics, such as numbers of installs and sessions, through App Store Connect. Apple decides what these contain and handles them under its own privacy policy. You can change this at any time in iPhone Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements.

7. What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising, ad networks or data brokers.
  • No sale of personal data, and no sharing for advertising.
  • In 1.1.0, no uploading of your photos.

8. Features not currently offered

In version 1.1.0, AI meal photo estimation is disabled and meal photos are not sent for analysis. Earlier versions of this policy described an optional Photo Estimate workflow. In an earlier version or test build that offers it, a fresh estimate requires confirmation before a compressed meal photo and technical request information are sent through CycleBalance’s meal-analysis service to Google Gemini. This is separate from your saved health diary and local reuse of a previously reviewed meal. Disabling the feature does not establish that records from earlier requests have been deleted. Contact us about the handling or retention of an earlier request; this update does not extend any previously stated retention period.

9. Exports and reports you share

You can create PDF reports and export or back up your data. These files are created on your device and go only where you send them, for example by email or AirDrop. Once shared, the recipient's handling of the file is outside our control.

10. The Website

The Website does not use cookies, advertising trackers or analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, which processes technical data such as IP addresses to deliver pages and keep the service secure. If you contact us through a form, your name, email address and message are delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co); we use them only to reply and delete them when no longer needed.

Some Website pages load fonts from Google Fonts. When this happens, your browser sends technical information, including your IP address and browser details, to Google to retrieve the fonts.

Provider privacy information: GitHub · FormSubmit · Google.

11. Your rights

You control locally stored health logs through the App, its export and deletion tools, and your device settings. For information we or our service providers hold, such as support messages or subscription records, you may contact contact@cyclebalance.app to request access, correction or deletion. Depending on applicable law, you may also have rights to portability, restriction, objection, withdrawal of consent and a complaint to a supervisory authority. These rights may arise under EU or UK GDPR, Swiss data protection law, or California law where applicable. We respond within the time required by applicable law and may need to verify your request. Our legal bases, where required, include performance of a contract for purchased features and legitimate interests in responding to support requests and administering subscriptions. We do not sell personal information or share it for advertising.

12. International transfers

Service providers may process information outside your country, including in the United States. RevenueCat’s data processing agreement describes Standard Contractual Clauses for covered restricted transfers. Other providers’ locations and practices are described in their own privacy information; we do not represent that the same transfer mechanism applies to every provider. Contact us for more information about a particular service.

13. Children

CycleBalance is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

14. Changes

We will post any changes on this page and update the date above. Material changes will also be noted in the App's release notes.
