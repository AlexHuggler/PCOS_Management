# CycleBalance 1.0.5 Localized Metadata

Status: exact staging copy only. Nothing in this file has been entered in App Store Connect. Native-speaker/legal review remains required for the six non-English localizations. These release notes describe the fail-closed `1.0.5 (18)` candidate: Photo Estimate remains hidden until its benchmark, privacy, profile, RevenueCat, screenshot, and approval gates pass.

Replace the existing absolute on-device/no-cloud paragraph in each storefront description with the corresponding paragraph below. Do not combine the old paragraph and replacement.

## en-US

### Release Notes

CycleBalance 1.0.5 strengthens meal logging and release safeguards. Photo Estimate remains hidden while quality, privacy, and distribution-profile gates are completed. Barcode scanning and manual nutrition entry remain available, with reliability and privacy-disclosure improvements.

### Replacement Description Paragraph

CycleBalance keeps saved health and nutrition logs on your device by default. Optional services are user initiated: Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases, barcode lookup sends only the selected UPC/EAN to public food databases, and—only when Photo Estimate is enabled and you confirm each upload—one compressed meal photo is sent through CycleBalance's secure service to Google Gemini for an editable estimate. The CycleBalance proxy does not retain raw photo bytes; Google may retain request content for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. See the Privacy Policy for cache, quota, and retention details.
