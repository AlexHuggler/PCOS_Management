## en-US

### Release Notes

CycleBalance 1.0.5 adds optional Photo Estimate for editable meal drafts. Every fresh upload requires confirmation for Google Gemini; paid access includes 10 fresh AI analyses in any rolling 24 hours, while exact cached repeats, barcode scanning, and manual entry use no AI quota. The CycleBalance proxy does not retain raw photos; Google may retain request content for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. This release also improves reliability and privacy disclosures.

### Replacement Description Paragraph

CycleBalance keeps saved health and nutrition logs on your device by default. Optional services are user initiated: Apple and RevenueCat handle purchases, barcode lookup sends only the selected UPC/EAN to Open Food Facts, and—only when you confirm a fresh Photo Estimate—one compressed meal photo is sent through CycleBalance's secure service to Google Gemini for an editable estimate. The CycleBalance proxy does not retain raw photo bytes; Google may retain request content for up to 55 days for abuse monitoring. Paid access includes 10 fresh analyses in any rolling 24 hours; exact cached repeats, barcode lookup, and manual entry do not consume AI quota. See the Privacy Policy for cache, quota, and retention details.
