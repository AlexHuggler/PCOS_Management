# CycleBalance Meal Scan Social/Demo Handoff

Status: **staged only — do not publish until scanner release verification and launch-delta approval are complete**

This packet is a production handoff for a privacy-safe product demonstration. It does not authorize posting, paid distribution, outreach, or claims about public availability.

## Required demonstration label

Every asset that uses a synthetic meal, generated interface state, or fixture record must keep this readable on-frame label from first appearance through the final frame:

> Demonstration using synthetic meal details — not a testimonial or accuracy result.

Do not present synthetic fixtures as customer data, a testimonial, a measured outcome, or evidence of scanner performance. Use only staged UI captured from a release-candidate build after the UI and copy match the verified release.

## 15-second Photo → Consent → Correct → Save storyboard

Format: 9:16 master, center-safe for 1:1 and 4:5 crops, captions burned in, sound optional. Keep the staged-release label visible in the opening and closing frames.

### Voiceover option

“Start with a photo. Choose whether to send it. Correct the draft. Save only what you reviewed.”

The voiceover must not promise a result, use diagnostic language, or say the scanner is available before release verification.

## Organic concept 1 — What the AI guessed—and what I corrected

- Hook: show a synthetic draft and three visible edits, never a percentage score.
- Sequence: guessed food label → corrected label → adjusted portion → added hidden ingredient → reviewed local save.
- Caption frame: `A photo starts the draft. My review makes the record.`
- Trust note: editing is expected safety behavior; do not frame corrections as a benchmark.
- CTA after release verification: link to the approved Campaign Link/Custom Product Page route from the synchronized launch handoff.

## Organic concept 2 — What a meal photo cannot see

- Hook: `The camera cannot see the dressing under this bowl.`
- Sequence: synthetic plate → off-frame preparation callouts → editable hidden-ingredient prompt → user correction.
- Cover examples: oil, sauce, preparation method, ingredient amount, substitutions, and food outside the frame.
- Caption frame: `A starting point—not a verdict on your meal.`
- Avoid moral language such as “good,” “bad,” “clean,” “cheat,” or “balanced score.”

## Organic concept 3 — Same meal again? Reuse your reviewed version

- Hook: show a previously reviewed synthetic meal card on-device.
- Sequence: select exact reviewed version → change today's one difference → save locally.
- Caption frame: `Reuse what you already reviewed.`
- State clearly: exact reviewed-meal reuse can remain on device and does not use a fresh scan.
- Never imply that a visually similar but different meal is automatically identical.

## Organic concept 4 — Context, not conclusions

- Hook: reviewed meal followed by an optional energy/fullness/cravings/digestion check-in.
- Sequence: save → optional context prompt → simple timeline placement.
- Caption frame: `Keep the context you want. Leave the conclusions to you and your care team.`
- Do not connect one meal to a symptom, glucose change, cycle event, or health outcome as cause and effect.

## Privacy-safe fixture rules

Use:

- a synthetic meal photo created for the demo or a licensed internal fixture with no person or identifying environment;
- invented generic food labels and clearly non-production fixture values;
- a blank or synthetic app profile;
- the required demonstration label on every applicable scene;
- a flattened export with metadata removed;
- a final manual review of every visible screen, notification, status bar, and background.

Never include:

- raw personal health data or a real meal record;
- a real username, email address, avatar, date, location, notification, photo-library thumbnail, or device name;
- symptoms, glucose, cycle phase, medications, supplements, notes, or clinician details from a real account;
- photo metadata or live gallery access;
- provider credentials, request IDs, quota identifiers, debug text, or internal endpoints;
- testimonials, star ratings, before/after framing, accuracy percentages, or model-comparison claims.

## Preflight gate

Before any export is approved for posting:

1. Confirm the scanner has passed release verification and is publicly available in the linked build.
2. Confirm the consent and retention disclosure matches the synchronized policy/store surfaces.
3. Confirm the current staged UI matches the final archived build.
4. Confirm the required synthetic-demonstration label is readable at normal playback speed.
5. Confirm no private or identifying data appears frame-by-frame.
6. Confirm captions and voiceover make no medical, causal, precision, or availability claims.
7. Confirm the final URL uses the approved Campaign Link route; do not use an untracked generic link in a campaign asset.

Owner approval required: Product, Privacy/Policy, App Store release owner, and Marketing.
