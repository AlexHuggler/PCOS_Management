A review-first loop

From plate to pattern-ready record.

The photo starts the draft. You control consent, corrections, saving, and any context you add afterward.

  1. Photo

    Choose a fresh meal photo when you want a starting point. Nothing is sent automatically.

  2. Consent

    See the provider disclosure and give explicit consent before a fresh upload goes to Google Gemini.

  3. Correct

    Review the editable draft. User correction comes before save, including foods, portions, and hidden ingredients.

  4. Save

    Keep the reviewed version as a local save only when it reflects what you want in your record.

  5. Context

    Optionally add a check-in such as energy, fullness, cravings, digestion, or another note that matters to you.

Questions before the release

What this preview means.

Is photo meal scanning available in the public app now?

No. This is a staged preview. The scanner is coming after release verification; the App Store links on this page lead to the current CycleBalance app.

Does choosing a photo save a meal automatically?

No. A fresh upload requires your consent, the returned draft stays editable, and nothing becomes a local meal record until you review and save it.

What can a meal photo miss?

Preparation details, oils, sauces, ingredient amounts, substitutions, and anything outside the frame. The draft is designed to be corrected.

Can I log without sending a photo?

Yes. Manual entry and exact reviewed-meal reuse can stay on your device. Barcode lookup is a separate optional network request and does not send a meal photo.

Where can I read the current policies?

See the current Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. Scanner-specific policy changes will be synchronized only after release verification.
